Refunds and cancellation

Effective Date: 26 November 2026 · Last Updated: 26 November 2026

We believe in transparent business relationships and treating every client fairly. This policy gives clarity about cancellations, project payments and the circumstances in which refunds may be available. Eligibility depends on the nature of the service, the stage of project completion, the applicable agreement and consumer protection laws.

1. Introduction

At Vintage Advertising, we are committed to providing professional digital services and maintaining fair, transparent relationships with our clients.

This Refund Policy explains the circumstances under which payments may be refunded, how cancellations are handled and what clients should expect when requesting a refund.

By purchasing our services, you acknowledge that you have had an opportunity to review the applicable service description, quotation and agreement.

Nothing in this policy is intended to restrict any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

2. General Refund Principles

Refund requests are assessed according to:

  • The service purchased.
  • The amount of work already completed.
  • The project stage.
  • Any third-party costs incurred.
  • The applicable service agreement.
  • Any mandatory consumer protection rights.

We aim to resolve refund requests fairly and promptly.

3. Website Development Projects

Website development projects involve planning, design, development, testing and implementation.

Where a client cancels a project:

  • Before work begins, payments may be refundable, subject to applicable legal rights and any valid, disclosed costs already incurred.
  • After work has commenced, the value of completed work and any properly incurred costs may be considered when determining the amount refundable, except where mandatory law requires a different outcome.
  • Once a project has been completed and delivered, refunds are generally not available merely because a client changes their mind, subject always to statutory rights and any failure by Vintage Advertising to meet its contractual obligations.

If a delivered website contains an issue that falls within the agreed project scope, we will assess the issue and determine an appropriate remedy, which may include correction or another remedy required by law.

4. Digital Marketing Services

Digital marketing services may include social media management, content creation, campaign management, advertising strategy and related services.

For monthly marketing subscriptions:

  • Subscription fees cover the agreed services and billing period.
  • Clients may request cancellation of future billing in accordance with their service agreement.
  • Payments for services already delivered are generally non-refundable, subject to applicable law.
  • Undelivered prepaid services will be assessed for an appropriate refund or credit.
  • Advertising budgets paid to third-party platforms are subject to the relevant platform’s policies and are not automatically refundable by Vintage Advertising.

5. AI Chatbots and Automation

AI chatbot development and automation projects may involve configuration, integration, testing, development and third-party software costs.

Refund requests are assessed based on the project stage and contractual obligations.

Third-party API usage, subscriptions, hosting charges and other external costs may be non-refundable where those costs have genuinely been incurred and are legally chargeable to the client.

Where a solution does not meet the agreed specifications, we will assess the issue and seek an appropriate resolution.

6. Deposits and Advance Payments

Some projects require an advance payment or deposit before work begins.

The treatment of deposits depends on the agreement, work undertaken, costs incurred and applicable consumer rights.

A deposit must not automatically be treated as non-refundable where applicable law provides otherwise.

7. Cancellation Requests

Clients who wish to cancel a project or subscription should contact us in writing.

Cancellation requests should include:

  • Client or business name.
  • Service purchased.
  • Invoice or transaction reference.
  • Reason for cancellation.
  • Relevant supporting information.

We will acknowledge the request and explain the next steps.

8. Statutory Cooling-Off Rights

For eligible electronic transactions, Zimbabwe’s Consumer Protection Act [Chapter 14:44], including section 53, provides a seven-day cooling-off right to cancel without reason or penalty, subject to the Act’s provisions.

Where that right applies, payments made must be refunded within the legally prescribed period.

This policy does not remove or reduce any statutory cancellation or refund rights.

9. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed through an appropriate payment method.

The time required for funds to appear in a client’s account may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

Where a statutory refund deadline applies, we will comply with that deadline.

10. Disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

We encourage clients to resolve concerns through direct communication before pursuing other available dispute-resolution options.

Nothing in this section prevents a client from exercising their legal rights.

12. Policy Updates

Vintage Advertising reserves the right to update this policy when necessary.

Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

The policy applicable to an existing transaction will be considered alongside the agreement in force when the transaction was entered into and any applicable legal requirements.
