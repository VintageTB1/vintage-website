FAQ

Questions,answered plainly.

How do payments work?

Marketing is paid monthly in advance. Websites and chatbot projects are paid as a 45% deposit, with the rest at an agreed milestone or before handover. Final payment methods are confirmed on your quote.

How long does a project take?

A starter website usually takes two to four weeks once we have your content. Marketing starts within a week of sign-off. Timelines are written into your quote.

How many revisions do I get?

Each package lists its revision rounds. A round is one consolidated set of changes. Extra rounds are quoted before we start them.

What is your refund policy?

Deposits cover work already started. If we cannot deliver what the contract describes, we discuss a fair refund. The full policy is on the Refund Policy page.

Can I cancel?

Marketing plans can be cancelled with notice before the next billing month. Projects follow the cancellation terms in your agreement.
