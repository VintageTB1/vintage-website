Cookie Policy

Effective Date: 26 November 2026 · Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Introduction

Vintage Advertising uses cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, improve performance and understand how visitors interact with our website.

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how they may be used and how you can manage your preferences.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

They help websites remember information about visits, preferences and interactions.

3. Types of Cookies

Our website may use the following categories:

Essential Cookies

These support necessary website functionality, security and navigation.

Functional Cookies

These remember user preferences and improve the browsing experience.

Analytics Cookies

These help us understand website traffic, visitor behaviour and performance.

Marketing Cookies

Where implemented and permitted, these may help measure advertising performance and provide relevant marketing content.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services integrated into our website may use their own cookies or similar technologies.

These may include analytics, advertising, embedded content and other external services.

Their use is governed by the relevant third-party privacy policies.

5. Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect certain website features.

Where required, our website will provide a cookie preference mechanism for managing non-essential cookies.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our website and technology change.

Updates will be published on this page.
