Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 26 November 2026 · Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Introduction

Vintage Advertising respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information collected through our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process and protect personal information.

Our approach is intended to align with applicable data protection legislation, including Zimbabwe’s Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07].

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

Personal information

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone or WhatsApp number.
  • Business or organisation name.

Project information

  • Website and business requirements.
  • Marketing objectives.
  • Project specifications.
  • Communication and consultation records.

Technical information

  • IP address.
  • Browser type.
  • Device information.
  • Website usage information.
  • Cookies and similar technologies, where applicable.

Transaction information

  • Invoice details.
  • Payment status.
  • Transaction references.
  • Billing information.

We do not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Respond to enquiries.
  • Prepare quotations and proposals.
  • Provide requested services.
  • Manage client accounts and projects.
  • Process payments and issue invoices.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Send relevant service communications.
  • Prevent fraud and protect our systems.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

Marketing communications will be handled in accordance with applicable consent and direct-marketing requirements.

4. AI Chatbots and Automated Processing

Vintage Advertising may use AI-powered systems to assist with enquiries, customer communication and business automation.

Where these systems process personal information, we aim to limit processing to what is necessary for the intended purpose.

Information submitted through AI-powered systems may be processed by external technology providers.

We will take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place when using these services.

Please avoid submitting passwords, payment card details or unnecessary sensitive personal information through chatbot conversations.

5. Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared with trusted service providers where necessary to operate our business, including:

  • Website hosting providers.
  • Email service providers.
  • Payment processors.
  • Database and infrastructure providers.
  • AI and API providers.
  • Analytics and security providers.

Such providers should only receive information necessary for their assigned functions, subject to applicable agreements and legal requirements.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or a valid legal process.

6. International Data Transfers

Some of our technology providers may process information outside Zimbabwe.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will take steps to comply with applicable data protection requirements and implement appropriate safeguards.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against:

  • Unauthorised access.
  • Unlawful processing.
  • Accidental loss.
  • Destruction.
  • Alteration.
  • Unauthorised disclosure.

No internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including contractual, administrative, accounting and legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable retention obligations.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally appropriate.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Exercise applicable rights concerning direct marketing.
  • Raise concerns about how your information is handled.

Requests may be subject to identity verification and applicable legal limitations.

10. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Support essential website functionality.
  • Remember preferences.
  • Understand website performance.
  • Improve the user experience.
  • Measure marketing effectiveness, where applicable.

You may manage cookies through your browser settings and any cookie preference controls provided on our website.

Non-essential tracking technologies will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

11. External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

Visitors should review the privacy policies of third-party services they use.

12. Children’s Privacy

Our services are primarily intended for businesses and adults.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection would be unlawful.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change.

The latest version will be published on this page.
