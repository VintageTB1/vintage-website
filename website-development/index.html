Questions

Good to know.

Do you build online stores?

Yes. The Premium website package includes an online store or booking.

Is hosting included?

Domain and hosting are quoted separately from the website build.

Can you improve my existing website?

Yes. Tell us what you have and what is not working, and we will recommend whether to improve it or rebuild it.

How do payments work?

Marketing is paid monthly in advance. Websites and chatbot projects are paid as a 45% deposit, with the rest at an agreed milestone or before handover. Final payment methods are confirmed on your quote.

How long does a project take?

A starter website usually takes two to four weeks once we have your content. Marketing starts within a week of sign-off. Timelines are written into your quote.

How many revisions do I get?

Each package lists its revision rounds. A round is one consolidated set of changes. Extra rounds are quoted before we start them.

Ready to buildsomething worth visiting?

Tell us about your business and what you want to achieve. We reply within two working days with next steps.
