How payment works

Simple, andby bank transfer.

  • Payment is by bank transfer only. We do not take card or mobile wallet payments.
  • A 45% deposit starts a project. The balance is due on the milestones set out in your quote.
  • Care plans are optional, paid monthly in advance, and can be cancelled before the next billing date. See care plans.
  • Bank details are shown inside your client portal when an invoice is issued, never in public.
  • An invoice is marked paid only after our team has verified the funds in the account. Uploading a receipt does not mark it paid.

Final prices depend on scope and are confirmed in your written quote. Use the pricing estimator for a quick indication.
