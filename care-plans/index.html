What we look after

Updates, fixesand support.

  • Text and photo updates
  • New pages, listings or products
  • Fixes when something stops working
  • Security updates

The number of changes included each month and our response times are set out in your quote, so you know exactly what your plan covers before you agree to it.

How it works

Simple tostart and stop.

  1. Onboarding. You tell us what we are looking after, who may send us change requests and who to call if the site goes down.
  2. Safe access. You never send us passwords. You add Vintage Advertising as an admin or collaborator on your own accounts, which stay in your name.
  3. Monthly billing. Care plans are paid monthly in advance, by bank transfer, with an invoice in your client portal.
  4. Cancel any time. Give written notice before the next billing date. The month already paid for is not refunded. See the refund policy.

Domain and hosting are quoted separately from care plans.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a care plan?

No. Care plans are optional. Your website or chatbot is yours to run without one, and you can add a plan at any time.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Give written notice before your next billing date. The month already paid for is not refunded, and the plan continues until that month ends.

What if I need more changes than my plan includes?

Extra work is quoted separately and only starts once you have approved the quote.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer only, monthly in advance. Your invoice and bank details are in your client portal. An invoice is marked paid after we confirm the funds.
